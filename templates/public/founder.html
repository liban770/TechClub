OUR STRATEGIC ROADMAP

From a Local Community to a Connected Technology Ecosystem

TechClub aims to grow from a focused community in Hargeisa into a sustainable network that creates meaningful opportunities for young people across Somaliland and beyond.

flag Phased Growth Framework (Future Strategic Goals)
STAGE 1 group_add

Build the Community

Bring together technology learners, developers, students, professionals, and enthusiasts.

Organize networking events, discussions, workshops, and opportunities for members to connect.

• Foundations in Hargeisa
STAGE 2 construction

Create Practical Opportunities

Encourage collaborative projects, technical workshops, mentorship, competitions, knowledge sharing, and professional development.

Help members turn their learning into practical, portfolio-grade experience.

• Practical Execution & Mentorship
STAGE 3 laptop_chromebook

Develop a Strong Platform

Develop a reliable digital platform where members can discover events, connect with others, showcase projects, and share opportunities.

Enable participation in community activities with transparent, structured workflows.

• Scalable Digital Infrastructure
STAGE 4 handshake

Build Partnerships & Expand

Establish meaningful relationships with universities, technology companies, startups, and potential sponsors.

Begin with a strong foundation in Hargeisa, expand across Somaliland, and build connections with wider Somali and international communities.

• Regional & Diaspora Reach
handshake STRATEGIC COLLABORATION

Let's Build the Future of Technology Together

TechClub welcomes collaboration with organizations and individuals who share our commitment to technology education, youth development, innovation, and professional growth.

We aim to build partnerships that create practical value for community members through learning opportunities, workshops, networking events, mentorship, competitions, and collaborative initiatives.

Together, we can help young people strengthen their skills, build meaningful connections, and contribute to the growing technology ecosystem.
