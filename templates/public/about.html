The Founding Narrative

Bridging Potential with Production-Grade Rigor.

Across the Horn of Africa, thousands of ambitious young builders are teaching themselves full-stack software development, distributed systems, machine learning, and UX engineering. Yet far too many work in isolation — without production code reviews, senior architectural guidance, or access to high-trust professional networks.

Founded in 2026, TechClub was created to eliminate that isolation. We provide the institutional foundation for Hargeisa's burgeoning tech cluster: an ecosystem that vets members, facilitates high-impact architecture workshops, coordinates 1-on-1 mentorship pairings, and curates an authenticated talent directory.
