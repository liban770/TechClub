TechClub Community Code of Conduct

Connect. Learn. Build Together. — Hargeisa Node

verified Every applicant must review and accept these core community standards before admission.

1. Respect Everyone

Treat every member with kindness, respect, and professionalism regardless of age, gender, background, technical experience, or education. Disagreements over technical architecture are acceptable; personal insults, bullying, and humiliation are strictly forbidden.

2. Maintain Professional Behavior

Communicate politely during meetups, architecture labs, hackathons, and online communication channels. Value other people's time, avoid unnecessary interruptions, and provide constructive feedback respectfully.

3. Support Learning & Knowledge Sharing

Welcome beginners and encourage technical curiosity. Experienced engineers and mentors are expected to uplift emerging learners. Never make peers feel inferior because of gaps in domain knowledge.

4. Protect Privacy & Member Confidentiality

Do not disclose or republish members' contact numbers, emails, proprietary project repositories, or private mentoring discussions without explicit permission. Member contact details remain confidential by default.

5. Honesty & Academic/Professional Integrity

Represent your technical background and qualifications truthfully. Respect intellectual property, open-source software licenses, and give credit to original creators.

6. Zero Tolerance for Harassment & Discrimination

Unwanted advances, discrimination, intimidation, stalking, offensive jokes, and spamming commercial promotions without approval will result in immediate suspension and permanent removal.

7. Accountability & Organizer Enforcement

Community organizers investigate reported violations impartially. Organizers have the full authority to issue formal warnings, temporarily suspend accounts, or permanently ban violators from all community spaces.
