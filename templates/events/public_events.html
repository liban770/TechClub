Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining TechClub workshops, meetups, and masterclasses.

payments Are TechClub events free or paid?

Community workshops, tech talks, and open meetups are completely free. Advanced masterclasses and special hackathons are sponsored by our partner network.

verified Do attendees get certificates?

Yes! Every attendee whose attendance is verified (via entrance QR scan for in-person gatherings or via the 1-click Join Live link for virtual classes) receives an official verifiable Certificate of Attendance.

group_add Do I need to be a TechClub member to register?

Public events are open to all passionate developers and students. Certain specialized masterclasses and restricted curriculum materials are reserved for verified TechClub Community Members.

laptop_mac What should I bring to hands-on workshops?

Bring your laptop, charger, and an eagerness to build! Course slides, starter templates, and workbooks can be downloaded in advance directly from the event details page.
