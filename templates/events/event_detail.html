workspace_premium When and how do I receive my Certificate of Attendance? expand_more

Official cryptographic certificates are automatically issued once your attendance is confirmed:

  • In-Person: Organizers scan your digital ticket QR code at the hub reception.
  • Online Live Class: Clicking the verified "Join Live Class" link from your digital ticket automatically verifies your attendance and unlocks your certificate instantly.

✓ Certificates include a unique verification ID and shareable credential link for LinkedIn and resumes.

folder_zip How do I access slides, workbooks, and downloadable resources? expand_more

Materials uploaded by instructors and organizers are available in the Course Materials & Downloads section above and directly on your Digital Ticket Pass:

• Public Resources: Accessible to anyone who has registered for this gathering.

• Members-Only Resources: Restricted to verified TechClub Community Members. If you are a guest, you can submit a community application anytime to unlock full member archives.

event_seat What happens if the gathering is Full? (Waitlist Policy) expand_more

When capacity ({{ event.capacity }} seats) is reached, new registrants join our Priority Waitlist. If a confirmed participant cancels their registration or venue capacity is increased, waitlisted members are automatically bumped up to confirmed status in FIFO order.

cancel Can I cancel my ticket or transfer it to someone else? expand_more

Digital tickets are issued to your registered name and email to maintain certificate validity. If your plans change and you cannot make it, please contact organizers or cancel via your ticket pass so your seat can be offered to waiting community members.
