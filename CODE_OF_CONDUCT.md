# TECHClub

## Community Code of Conduct

**Connect. Learn. Build Together.**

### 1. Respect Everyone

* Treat every member with respect, kindness, and professionalism, regardless of their age, gender, background, education, experience, or technical ability.
* Respect different opinions, ideas, cultures, and perspectives.
* Never insult, humiliate, bully, threaten, or discriminate against another member.
* Disagreements are acceptable; personal attacks are not.

### 2. Maintain Professional Behavior

* Communicate politely and professionally during meetings, events, discussions, and online conversations.
* Respect other people's time and avoid unnecessary interruptions.
* Listen when others are speaking and give everyone an opportunity to participate.
* Accept constructive criticism and provide feedback respectfully.

### 3. Support Learning and Knowledge Sharing

* Help beginners feel welcome and encourage them to ask questions.
* Share knowledge, useful resources, opportunities, and experiences whenever possible.
* Never make someone feel inferior because they lack experience or technical knowledge.
* Recognize that everyone has something valuable to teach and learn.

### 4. Respect Different Ideas and Opinions

* Discuss ideas based on evidence, reasoning, and constructive arguments.
* Do not force others to agree with your opinions.
* Welcome healthy debates about technology, innovation, and professional development.
* If a disagreement becomes personal or disrespectful, stop and seek help from an organizer when necessary.

### 5. Protect Privacy and Confidentiality

* Do not share another member's personal information, private messages, photographs, or contact details without permission.
* Do not record meetings, take photographs, or publish identifiable content without appropriate consent.
* Respect confidential information shared during mentoring sessions, meetings, or community activities.
* Never misuse community members' information for harassment, spam, scams, or unauthorized promotion.

### 6. Be Honest and Responsible

* Represent your qualifications, skills, experience, and achievements honestly.
* Do not plagiarize other people's work or claim their ideas as your own.
* Give proper credit when using another person's work, code, research, or creative ideas.
* Respect intellectual property, software licenses, and the contributions of others.
* Take responsibility for your actions and correct mistakes when necessary.

### 7. Maintain Appropriate Professional Boundaries

* Respect personal boundaries and other members' comfort.
* Do not pressure members into personal relationships, financial transactions, business deals, or activities they do not want to join.
* Harassment, unwanted advances, intimidation, and inappropriate behavior are not acceptable.
* Keep mentoring, networking, and collaboration respectful and voluntary.

### 8. Participate Positively in Events

* Arrive on time and inform the organizer if you cannot attend a confirmed event.
* Follow event instructions and respect speakers, mentors, guests, and fellow participants.
* Ask relevant questions and contribute constructively.
* Avoid disrupting presentations, workshops, or networking activities.
* Leave shared spaces clean and treat equipment and facilities responsibly.

### 9. Use Technology and Community Platforms Responsibly

* Do not share harmful, illegal, misleading, or abusive content through community channels.
* Do not spam members with advertisements, unsolicited promotions, or repeated messages.
* Never attempt to access another member's account, data, or systems without authorization.
* Do not use TechClub to promote scams, fraud, cyber abuse, or malicious software.
* Keep discussions relevant to technology, learning, networking, innovation, and professional development.

### 10. Encourage Collaboration, Not Competition

* Celebrate the achievements and progress of other members.
* Share opportunities fairly and avoid deliberately preventing others from learning or participating.
* Give credit to everyone who contributes to a project.
* Build relationships based on trust, cooperation, and mutual benefit.
* Remember that TechClub exists to help the community grow together.

### 11. Avoid Conflicts of Interest and Misuse of the Community

* Do not use your position, influence, or access to member information for personal advantage at the expense of others.
* Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when making community decisions.
* Do not falsely represent yourself as an official TechClub representative.
* Obtain authorization before making official commitments or speaking on behalf of the community.
* Promotions, partnerships, and fundraising activities must follow the organizers' guidelines.

### 12. Report Concerns and Resolve Problems

* Members are encouraged to report harassment, discrimination, misconduct, privacy violations, or other serious concerns to the organizers.
* Reports should be handled respectfully, discreetly, and fairly.
* Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
* Members should cooperate honestly with reasonable reviews of reported incidents.
* Organizers should assess concerns fairly and take appropriate action.

### 13. Consequences for Violating the Rules

Depending on the seriousness and frequency of the violation, the organizers may:

1. Give a private reminder or informal warning.
2. Issue a formal warning.
3. Temporarily restrict participation in community activities or online spaces.
4. Suspend membership or event participation.
5. Remove membership for serious or repeated violations.

Serious misconduct may justify immediate restrictions while the organizers assess the situation. Decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and handled fairly. Members should have a reasonable opportunity to explain their side of the situation.

### 14. Responsibilities of Organizers

* Lead by example and follow the same rules as other members.
* Treat applications, complaints, and membership decisions fairly.
* Protect members' personal information.
* Apply community rules consistently without favoritism.
* Communicate important decisions clearly and respectfully.
* Help create an inclusive environment where members feel safe to learn, network, and collaborate.

---

## Our Shared Commitment

TechClub is more than a technology club. It is a community built on respect, trust, learning, collaboration, and opportunity.

Every member is responsible for helping create an environment where beginners feel welcome, professionals feel respected, ideas can be shared freely, and everyone has the opportunity to grow.

**Connect with respect. Learn with humility. Build with integrity. Grow together.**

By joining TechClub, members agree to respect this Code of Conduct and contribute positively to the community.
